FUNCTIONING FAITH

Privacy Policy

Version 2026-08-11 · Last updated August 11, 2026

Information we process

We process account details, age confirmation, profile and audience choices, posts, comments, groups, reports, messages, workouts, achievements, notifications, device-session metadata, and security events. With separate opt-in consent, we process GPS, heart rate, stress, sleep, readiness, and connected fitness-platform data. Developer applicants also provide a verified education identity, church relationship, project purpose, attestations, and review history.

Why we process it

We use data to authenticate members, provide community and fitness features, apply privacy choices, prevent abuse, deliver requested notifications, match verified Scripture, sync selected services, review reports, and keep an audit trail for sensitive actions. We do not sell personal data.

Scripture and AI services

Gloo may receive the minimum context needed to select among verified Scripture candidates or assist a feature. YouVersion supplies or verifies Bible content. AI does not author Bible text. Biometric Scripture personalization is separate from biometric ingestion and can be disabled.

Location and health information

GPS and health data are sensitive. The web app processes them only for a workout or connector you initiate and only when required consent is active. Public route sharing is opt-in and route endpoints can be trimmed. Revoking biometric consent stops new biometric writes; retention and deletion requests remain available through account controls.

Audience and safety choices

You can set profile, follower-list, message, comment, post, and workout audiences; block, mute, or restrict accounts; review active sessions; remotely log out devices; and receive alerts about security-sensitive actions. Teen accounts receive stricter messaging rules.

Service providers and transfers

Selected features may use Google or Apple sign-in, Railway hosting, Gloo, YouVersion, YouTube/Vimeo, Strava, Fitbit, Oura, web-push providers, and an email provider if configured. Each receives only information needed for that feature and applies its own terms. Embedded media can disclose standard browser request information to its provider when played.

Protection and retention

Traffic is protected by HTTPS in production. Passwords are salted with scrypt; developer keys are hash-stored; MFA secrets are field-encrypted; session identifiers are signed and backed by revocable server records. Access controls, rate limits, moderation records, and security logs reduce misuse. Functioning Faith does not currently claim end-to-end encrypted DMs or whole-database application-layer encryption.

We retain data while an account or feature needs it, then delete or de-identify it subject to safety, fraud, dispute, backup, and legal-preservation needs. Security and moderation records may be retained longer when reasonably necessary.

Export, correction, and deletion

Profile Settings provides data export and account deletion. Deletion revokes sessions, credentials, connectors, public overlays, and user-linked records covered by the current data inventory. Some records may be preserved where required for safety or law, with access restricted. Visit Support for correction or privacy requests.

Children

The service is not offered to children under 13 and does not claim a COPPA parental-consent flow. If we learn that an under-13 account was created, we will disable it and review deletion. Teen protections do not replace parental guidance.

Changes and contact

Material policy changes are shown before continued community publishing. Use in-app reporting for safety issues, or visit Support for privacy questions. This policy should receive jurisdiction-specific legal review before broad commercial launch.
