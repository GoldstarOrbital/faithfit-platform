FUNCTIONING FAITH

Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-04 · Last updated October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Functioning Faith collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our website, iOS app, and connected fitness features. It applies to information linked to a Functioning Faith account. It does not replace the privacy notices of services you independently connect or content providers whose media you choose to play.

Information we collect

Account and profile information. We collect your email address, display name and username, password hash (not your plaintext password), date of birth or age confirmation, sign-in provider identity when you use Google, Microsoft, or Apple, profile photo, bio, profile links, privacy settings, and consent choices.

Community content. We collect posts, photos and videos you upload, stories, Reels, comments, reactions, saved items, follows, reports, group membership and messages, event RSVPs, workout invitations, and support requests. Content that you choose to make public or share with followers can be seen by the audience you select. Compatible direct-message participants may use end-to-end encryption after each has set up encryption keys; unencrypted messages, group messages, message metadata, and messages sent before that setup remain server-processed. Do not use any in-app message for highly sensitive information.

Fitness, location, and health information. When you start or log a workout, we process the activity type, start and end times, duration, distance, route points, pace or speed, elevation where available, calories, heart-rate metrics, workout notes, and achievements. With your separate permission, the iOS app can read and write selected Apple Health workout data, and you can connect supported services such as Strava, Fitbit, Oura, Google Health, or Spotify. The precise fields available depend on the service and permissions you grant. Bluetooth data is processed only when you choose to pair a compatible sensor.

Device, security, and usage information. We process active-session details, device or browser name, a hashed IP address for security events, sign-in and sensitive-account-change history, push subscription or device-token information when notifications are enabled, and a first-party random visit cookie described below. We do not use third-party advertising pixels or cross-site tracking cookies.

Verification and administrator information. If you apply as a verified developer or church representative, we process the education email, church relationship and public contact email, project purpose, required attestations, application and review history, submitted content, and enforcement records.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; deliver workouts, Scripture, social, group, safety, wearable, and notification features; honor visibility and blocking controls; personalize features you turn on; detect abuse and enforce our standards; respond to support requests; maintain records needed for security and legal compliance; and improve reliability. We do not sell personal information or use it for interest-based advertising.

Scripture and AI

Functioning Faith uses verified Bible sources to serve Scripture. YouVersion may supply or verify Bible content. When enabled, Gloo may receive the minimum request context needed for a Gloo-supported feature, such as a Bible question, selected Bible reference, tradition or translation setting, limited workout context, post text for a Scripture match, or group and church catalog information for a recommendation. AI does not author or rewrite Bible text. Gloo responses are only shown when the app can verify their Scripture references; failed verification is not presented as an answer. We cache limited verified results to make repeated requests faster and reduce service usage.

When information is shared

We share information with the audience and integrations you choose. Your workout, route, profile, followers, post, story, Reel, group activity, or athlete profile may be visible according to the audience setting you select. Route sharing is optional; the app can trim route endpoints, but you remain responsible for reviewing whether a route may reveal sensitive places. We disclose information to service providers that help run selected features, including hosting and database providers, email delivery, push delivery, Apple and Google sign-in, connected fitness providers, Gloo, YouVersion, YouTube/Vimeo, and map or church-search providers. Those providers receive information needed for the requested feature and may apply their own terms and privacy policies. Playing embedded media can disclose ordinary device or browser request information to that provider.

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to protect people, investigate abuse, enforce our Terms, comply with law, or respond to valid legal process. For a verified developer, a confirmed serious legal or policy violation may be reported to the verified church contact only after evidence review and notice, as described in the Terms. We do not report unreviewed allegations or automated scores as established facts.

Cookies and similar technology

We use necessary session cookies to keep you signed in and a random, first-party ff_vid cookie to count distinct website visitors. The visitor cookie contains no directly identifying information, is not used to follow you across other sites, and may be retained for up to 400 days. We do not use fingerprinting or third-party advertising analytics pixels.

Your choices

You can manage profile, follower-list, message, comment, tag, story, post, and workout audiences; block, mute, or restrict accounts; control notifications; disconnect supported services; revoke location, Health, Bluetooth, photo-library, and notification permissions through your device or browser; review active sessions; and remotely sign out devices. Turning off a permission stops new collection through that permission; it does not automatically erase information already recorded.

Retention, export, correction, and deletion

We retain information while your account or a feature needs it. You can request an export and permanently delete your account from Profile Settings. Deletion revokes sessions, credentials, connectors, and public overlays and removes user-linked records covered by the current data inventory. We may retain limited information when needed for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, backups, legal obligations, or a lawful preservation request, with access restricted. To request correction, privacy help, or additional information about applicable privacy rights, contact alexgoldsmith@goldstarorbital.com.

Children

Functioning Faith is not offered to children under 13 and does not provide a COPPA parental-consent flow. If we learn an under-13 account was created, we will disable it and review deletion. Teen accounts receive stricter defaults, but parental guidance remains important.

Security, changes, and contact

We use HTTPS in production, salted password hashing, signed and revocable sessions, access controls, rate limits, security logging, and encryption for selected secrets. No system can guarantee absolute security. We may update this policy as the product or law changes; material changes will be presented before continued community publishing where required. For any privacy, account, safety, or technical help, contact alexgoldsmith@goldstarorbital.com. In an emergency, contact local emergency services first.
