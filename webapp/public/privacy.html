FUNCTIONING FAITH

Privacy Policy

Last updated August 11, 2026

What we collect

We collect the information you choose to provide, including your name, email, profile details, workouts, posts, messages, group activity, and connected-account identifiers. If you opt in, the app may process location, heart-rate, and wearable data to provide fitness features.

How we use it

We use your information to operate your account, show the feed and communities you choose to join, record workouts, personalize Scripture and coaching when you enable that setting, and protect the service. We do not sell personal data.

Your choices

Workout visibility, route-end privacy, personalization, biometric ingestion, notifications, connected accounts, and group participation are controlled in the app. You can export your data from Profile → Settings. You can revoke optional consent at any time.

Location and health data

GPS and sensor data are used for the workout you start. They are not collected in the background by this web app. Public workout routes should use the route privacy trim before sharing.

Deletion and contact

To request account deletion or ask a privacy question, contact the Functioning Faith team through the support channel associated with your account. We will verify ownership before acting on a deletion request.

Third parties

Sign-in providers, YouVersion, Gloo, YouTube, Strava, and wearable connectors only receive information needed for the feature you choose to use. Their own privacy policies apply.
