FUNCTIONING FAITH

Privacy Policy

Effective September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Functioning Faith collects, uses, shares, retains, and protects information when you use our mobile app and website.

Information we process

We process account details, age confirmation, profile and audience choices, posts, comments, groups, reports, messages, workouts, achievements, notification preferences, device-session metadata, and security events. When you separately opt in, we process GPS, heart rate, stress, sleep, readiness, and data from connected fitness platforms. Developer applicants also provide a verified education identity, church relationship, project purpose, attestations, and review history.

Why we process it

We use information to authenticate members; provide community, fitness, and Scripture features; honor privacy choices; prevent abuse; deliver requested notifications; sync services you connect; review reports; and maintain an audit trail for sensitive actions. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Scripture and AI services

Gloo may receive the minimum context needed to select among verified Scripture candidates or assist a feature. YouVersion supplies or verifies Bible content. AI does not author Bible text. Biometric Scripture personalization is separate from biometric ingestion and can be disabled.

Location and health information

GPS and health data are sensitive. We process them only for a workout or connector you initiate and only while the required consent is active. Public route sharing is opt-in and route endpoints can be trimmed. Revoking biometric consent stops new biometric writes; account controls remain available for retention and deletion requests.

Audience and safety choices

You can set profile, follower-list, message, comment, post, and workout audiences; block, mute, or restrict accounts; review active sessions; remotely log out devices; and receive alerts about security-sensitive actions. Teen accounts receive stricter messaging rules.

Service providers and transfers

Selected features use Google or Apple sign-in, Railway hosting, Gloo, YouVersion, YouTube/Vimeo, Strava, Fitbit, Oura, web-push providers, and an email provider when you use the related feature. Each receives only information needed for that feature and applies its own terms. Embedded media can disclose standard browser request information to its provider when played.

Anonymous visit counting

Loading the app sets a random, first-party cookie (ff_vid) with no personal information in it, so we can count how many distinct people visit — including people who never create an account — separately from signed-in activity. No fingerprinting and no third-party analytics pixel are used for this. It does not track you across other sites.

Protection and retention

Traffic is protected by HTTPS in production. Passwords are salted with scrypt; developer keys are hash-stored; MFA secrets are field-encrypted; and session identifiers are signed and backed by revocable server records. Access controls, rate limits, moderation records, and security logs help reduce misuse. Functioning Faith does not claim end-to-end encrypted DMs or whole-database application-layer encryption.

We retain data while an account or feature needs it, then delete or de-identify it, subject to safety, fraud, dispute, backup, and legal-preservation needs. Security and moderation records may be retained longer when reasonably necessary.

Your choices and requests

Profile Settings provides data export and account deletion. You can also update profile and audience choices, notification preferences, and connected services there. Deletion revokes sessions, credentials, connectors, public overlays, and user-linked records covered by the current data inventory. Some records may be preserved where required for safety or law, with access restricted. For access, correction, deletion, or privacy questions, email hello@functioningfaith.app.

Children

The service is not offered to children under 13 and does not claim a COPPA parental-consent flow. If we learn that an under-13 account was created, we will disable it and review deletion. Teen protections do not replace parental guidance.

Changes and contact

We will post an updated effective date when this policy changes. For material changes, we will provide notice in the app or by another appropriate method before the change takes effect. Use in-app reporting for safety issues, or email hello@functioningfaith.app for privacy questions.
